About the Band

Born from the
Streets of Oxford

The Chase are an indie rock band from Oxford, UK — a city whose centuries of history, amber-lit cobblestones, and restless creative spirit runs through every note they play. Formed from a shared love of melody, wit, and raw guitar, the band have been part of Oxford's vibrant live music scene for years.

What started as a covers band — honing their craft across the pubs, arts venues, and late-night haunts of one of England's great musical cities — has evolved into something original, personal, and genuinely their own. The Chase now write and perform entirely original music: indie rock with a romantic streak and a clever edge.

Think punchy riffs and honest lyrics. Think late autumn in Oxford, warm light on wet stone, and songs that stay with you long after the last chord fades.

Songs that carry the weight of a city that's been dreaming out loud for eight hundred years.

Meet the Members

M
Matt
Vocals

The voice and lyrical centre of The Chase, Matt brings emotional depth and literary wit to every song. His writing draws from the romantic and the specific — stories of longing, connection, and the curious beauty of a city that never quite lets you go. On stage he's equal parts intensity and charm.

R
Rob
Drums & Backing Vocals

The rhythmic heartbeat of the band, Rob drives the Chase's sound with precision, feel, and a certain restless energy that keeps every song moving. His backing vocals add warmth and texture to the mix, transforming solo melodies into something bigger and more powerful than any single part.

A
Andy
Guitar

Andy's guitar work is the defining sonic signature of The Chase — versatile, inventive, and never predictable. Whether shaping a delicate melodic line or unleashing a full-tilt chorus riff, he brings clarity and craft to every moment, turning the spaces between notes into something you can feel.

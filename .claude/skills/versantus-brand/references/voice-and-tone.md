# Versantus — Voice & Tone

How Versantus writes. The visual brand is playful-yet-professional; the words match it.

> **Voice in one line:** Clear, honest, human and confident — we make the complex feel
> simple, and we sound like a smart, warm colleague, never a corporate brochure.

## Who we are (context for tone)

Versantus is an Oxford digital agency building "transformative digital solutions" —
world-class websites and software — for **ambitious** organisations, startups and
scale-ups. Founded to fill a gap for clear, honest, **jargon-free** software work.
Values: **quality, learning, collaboration, being good.** Purpose-driven and
sustainable; a multi-award team (UK Best Workplaces in Tech). People-first culture.

## Core principles

1. **Clear over clever.** Say the thing plainly. If a sentence needs re-reading, cut it.
2. **Jargon-free.** No "synergy", "leverage", "solutions-oriented", "best-in-class".
   Explain tech in human terms.
3. **Confident, not boastful.** State what we do and the outcome. Let results speak.
4. **Warm and collaborative.** "We", "you", "together", "your team". Partnership, not vendor.
5. **Playful in moments.** A light, witty turn is welcome — never forced, never cheesy.
6. **Outcome-led.** Tie features to what the user/client actually gets (faster, clearer,
   measurable, more sustainable).
7. **Honest.** No overclaiming. Specific beats superlative.

## Sentence patterns that sound like us

- Short, direct openers. "We build websites people love to use."
- Lead with the benefit, then the how. "Faster decisions, because the data is finally clear."
- Active voice, present tense. "We design", not "designs are created by us".
- A confident fragment for punch. "World-class digital. Built in Oxford."
- "Ambitious" is an on-brand word — use it for the kind of clients/work we love.

## Words we like / avoid

**Like:** ambitious, build, design, partner, clear, honest, world-class, measurable,
sustainable, transform, craft, together, thoughtful, real.

**Avoid:** synergy, leverage, cutting-edge (overused), bespoke (overused), seamless
(overused), revolutionary, ninja/guru/rockstar, "solutions" as filler, exclamation
overload.

## Punctuation & style

- British English spelling (organise, colour, optimise, personalise).
- Sentence case for headings (not Title Case, not ALL CAPS — except short eyebrows/labels).
- Oxford comma optional but be consistent.
- One exclamation mark at most, and rarely.
- Emoji: fine and on-brand for Instagram/LinkedIn in moderation; none in formal/UI copy.

## Social formats

### LinkedIn (professional, value-led, still warm)
- **Hook line** that states a benefit or a human truth (no clickbait).
- 2–4 short paragraphs / single-line breaks for readability.
- Concrete detail or outcome; credit the team or client where relevant.
- Soft CTA ("Read how →", "Say hello", "We're hiring →").
- 3–6 relevant hashtags, lower-key (e.g. #Drupal #UXDesign #Oxford #DigitalAgency).

### Instagram (warmer, more human, team & culture)
- Friendlier, first-person, a touch playful. Emoji ok (1–4).
- Celebrate people, behind-the-scenes, launches, learning, wins.
- Caption mirrors the on-image headline; keep it punchy.

### Caption skeletons
- *Launch:* "New site, live today. We worked with [client] to [outcome]. Here's what we built →"
- *Culture:* "Another reason we love it here: [thing]. 💜"
- *Insight:* "[Plain-English insight]. Here's why it matters for your [users/site/team]."
- *Hiring:* "We're growing. If you [trait], we should talk →"

## Before / after

| ❌ Off-brand | ✅ On-brand |
|---|---|
| "We leverage cutting-edge, best-in-class solutions to drive synergy." | "We build digital tools that are genuinely better to use — and easier to run." |
| "Versantus Ltd is a provider of bespoke software solutions." | "We're Versantus — an Oxford team building world-class websites and software." |
| "Our seamless, revolutionary platform disrupts the industry." | "Clearer data, faster decisions. That's the difference good design makes." |
| "Click here to learn more!!!" | "See how we did it →" |

## Tone dial (adjust to context)

- **Website / hero:** confident + warm, benefit-led, concise.
- **LinkedIn:** professional + human, value-first.
- **Instagram:** warm + playful, people-first.
- **Proposals / technical:** clear + precise, still jargon-free; explain, don't impress.
- **Recruitment:** open, honest, genuinely friendly.
